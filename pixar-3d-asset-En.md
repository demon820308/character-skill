### 7. FAQ & Troubleshooting

- **Q1: Why does the generated sheet show only equal-sized small figures without a standout hero standing pose?**
  - **A**: This is the classic "Thumbnail Parity Trap." Check Tier 2 of the prompt to see if the turnaround or multi-angle views were set to equal widths. Ensure Column 1 commands 38%~40% width and declares `A single, commanding, large-scale full-body hero presentation figure dominating the upper canvas as the primary visual anchor`, shrinking the technical turnaround into a compact secondary column.
- **Q2: Why do grey, uncanny-valley plaster busts appear on the board?**
  - **A**: Check if prompt keywords such as `clay render`, `AO clay`, or `wireframe topology` were included. Diffusion models cannot interpret 3D quad-mesh topology; they will only render rigid grey plaster or facial spiderwebs. Thoroughly remove such terms and use pure, full-color `MODEL SHEET`.
- **Q3: Why are there distorted English words printed next to the color swatches?**
  - **A**: Check if natural language color descriptions like `(espresso)` or `(warm jasmine)` were appended to the color palette definition. Ensure that only single-quoted 6-digit hex codes (such as `'#DFD1B3'`) appear to the right of the swatches, without any parenthetical explanations.
